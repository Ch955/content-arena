# Brief: Book story #1 — Betala Katha, the three over-delicate brothers

## Angle
Vikram–Betal frame (story ends with a question → viewers answer in comments). 5-second countdown after
Betal's question, then Vikram's answer and his reasoning ("proof you can see beats a claim someone could have
been told"), then "మీరు agree ఆ? comment చేయండి" + follow/subscribe.

## Our adaptations (not the source's words)
- Caste label dropped ("three brothers"). The father's ritual errand dropped.
- Rice smells of smoke: source says a cremation ground beside the field → we say a brick kiln.
- Second brother: source has a courtesan who smells of goat's milk → we recast as the king's perfume kept in a
  goatskin bag (smell test). This is our invention; the caption says "retold and adapted".
- Bed test kept as in the source: seven mattresses, a single hair under them, a red mark on his body by morning.
- Vikram's answer kept: the bed-fussy brother, because his proof was visible on his body while the other two
  might have been told beforehand.
