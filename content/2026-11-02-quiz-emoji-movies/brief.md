# Brief: Quiz — Guess the Telugu movie from emojis

## Angle
"Only real TFI fans get 4/4." Big emoji clue on each question slide, answer + one-line hint on the next.
Wrong options share the answer's director or lead actor (no rival-star fan war). Text + emoji only — no posters,
stills, songs, dialogues or star names in clues. Goal: comments ("score comment lo").

## Questions (in this order)
1. 💑 🔪 🪰 😤 — A) Vikramarkudu B) Maryada Ramanna C) Eega → **C) Eega** (2012, S. S. Rajamouli).
   Hint: a murdered young man is reborn as a housefly and takes revenge on his killer.
2. 🏏 👨‍👦 👕 ⏳ — A) Jersey B) Bhale Bhale Magadivoy C) Ninnu Kori → **A) Jersey** (2019, Gowtam Tinnanuri).
   Hint: a failed cricketer makes a comeback in his mid-30s to gift his son an India team jersey.
3. 🕰️ ⚙️ ⏪ 👑 — A) Bhairava Dweepam B) Aditya 369 C) Sri Krishnarjuna Vijayam → **B) Aditya 369**
   (1991, Singeetam Srinivasa Rao). Hint: a time machine takes the heroes back to Sri Krishnadevaraya's court —
   often called Indian cinema's first time-travel film.
4. 🎓 😩 💍 🎭 🏫 — A) Shavukaru B) Pelli Chesi Choodu C) Missamma → **C) Missamma** (1955, L. V. Prasad).
   Hint: two jobless graduates pretend to be married to get teaching jobs at a village school.
