## Premise options (the "would I send this to a friend?" test)
1. **(chosen) The forehead number at the engagement.** Every Telugu 25–34 viewer has heard "నీకెంత package?" at a
   function. The reel lets them watch the comparison turn around: the crore-package son has the smallest number, the
   cousin everyone pitied for farming has a huge one, and Ammamma (no salary) beats everyone. Send-to-friend test:
   **yes**. "Tag the cousin who always gets compared" is a compliment and a joke at once, and the "what's your
   number?" question is easy to answer and a little confessional, which is what drives comments.
2. **What if every message in the family WhatsApp group showed the sender's real mood?** ("Good morning 🌸" from
   Peddamma = 😤). Very funny and local, but it's a gag reel without a turn or a payoff that makes people rewatch.
3. **What if your appraisal rating were given by your Amma?** Cute role reversal, but it lands as a sweet sketch, not
   as an adult what-if; the twist is guessable from the title.

## Plot-hole check (the twist must survive a rewatch)
- **What exactly is counted?** Whole hours with family in 2026, phone down. In-person only: Rohit's video calls with
  his mother don't count, which is why the US son is at 12. The rule is spoken in L8 ("phone లేకుండా") and on screen.
- **Why does Nikhil's number tick right at the laddu?** The count is cumulative; he was at 96 and a bit, and the minutes
  since he walked in finished the hour while he sat with Amma. Ananya reads it as a link to Amma (the clue); it is also
  simply true that this was family time.
- **Why don't Srinu's or Ammamma's numbers tick in the same seconds?** Their hours didn't happen to finish in those few
  minutes. Every phone-down person ticks +3 in the 3-hour time-lapse, and only Rohit (on his phone until L10) and
  Ananya (not with her family) stay put before that. Rohit's "doesn't move" is the point of L6: he's the only one with a
  phone in his hand.
- **Ammamma has no salary but the biggest number**: the first fair clue that it isn't money. She lives with Srinu's
  family and is with them all day. (I made the counted group "family", not "parents" as in the premise, because a
  74-year-old's parents would be long gone and her biggest number would then be a plot hole.)
- **Srinu 2,400 vs Ammamma 4,500**: he's out in the fields part of the day; she is home all day.
- **Nikhil's "నాలుగు రోజులే"**: 97 ÷ 24 = 4.04 days, shown as a chip on screen. He says "Amma-Nanna తో" because his
  family time is almost all with them. (Tonight's function hour counts too; fine, it's family.)
- **Where does Nikhil live?** Not stated, on purpose. The 15 Nov reel has his parents' home in Kukatpally (he visits);
  the 16 Nov reel has him living with Amma. "Phone down" makes the sting work either way, so I dropped the manager's
  "same city" idea to avoid contradicting either reel.
- **The loop clue is real**: frame 1 (before any number appears) already shows Rohit as the only person on a phone,
  Srinu helping Ammamma, Amma holding a laddu, and Nikhil arriving late with his office lanyard.
- Ananya's 610 never moves and she leaves before the time-lapse, so nobody wonders why hers doesn't climb.
