# Brief: Quiz — Guess the Telugu movie from the clue

## Angle
"Meeru nijamga Tollywood fan aa?" — one clue per era, easy → hard. Text only (no posters, stills, songs,
lyrics, dialogues). Wrong options are films by the same director/star, so there is no rival-star fan war.
Goal: comments ("answers comment lo — A, B or C?"). Answers revealed on the slide right after each question.

## Questions (in this order)
1. 2022 · Dir. S. S. Rajamouli · its song became the first song from an Indian film to win the Oscar for
   Best Original Song (95th Academy Awards). A) Magadheera B) RRR C) Sye → **B) RRR** ("Naatu Naatu" — text only).
   Keep the exact wording "first song from an Indian film".
2. 2006 · Dir. Puri Jagannadh · remade in Tamil (2007) and in Hindi as "Wanted" (2009).
   A) Athadu B) Pokiri C) Okkadu → **B) Pokiri**
3. 1957 · Dir. K. V. Reddy · a fantasy based on a Mahabharata story · topped a 2013 CNN-IBN online poll of the
   greatest Indian films. A) Pathala Bhairavi B) Mayabazar C) Lava Kusa → **B) Mayabazar**
   (answer slide bonus: first Telugu film to be digitally colourised, re-released 2010)
4. 1980 · Dir. K. Viswanath · a story about a Carnatic music maestro · S. P. Balasubrahmanyam's first National
   Award for playback singing. A) Sagara Sangamam B) Swathi Kiranam C) Sankarabharanam → **C) Sankarabharanam**
   (no award year/ceremony number on the slide; no years for the wrong options)
