# Brief: Rasam Comedy Ep 6 — "One Punch, Ten Rowdies"

## Angle
Genre of the day (Monday): **action/mass**. Ep 1 already did the mass hero *entry*, so today we spoof
the mass *fight*: the trope where the hero throws **one punch** and **ten goons fly in slow motion**
in every direction, physics be damned. Our colony version: Pandu's "gang" of rowdies is a row of
**ten pillows and sofa cushions**, each wearing a tiny black cap like Pandu's. Chinna's slow-motion
punch hits only air, and every pillow flies, spinning softly, in glorious slow motion.

The twist goes to **Pinky** this time, not Amma (Amma's twist carried Ep 1–4 and she was the "canteen"
in Ep 5; she sits this one out). One of the flying "rowdies" was **Pinky's pink bunny pillow**, which
Pandu borrowed for his gang. Pinky walks up, tiny and furious, and blows **one small puff — "ఫూ!"** — at
Chinna… and the mass hero flies backwards in even bigger slow motion onto the soft pillow pile. The
real mass is the 5-year-old.

**Three angles considered**
1. **One punch, ten pillows… one puff, one hero** (chosen): *before* — "the hero is the most powerful
   guy in the fight"; *after* — "the most powerful person in every house is the little sister." Why they
   stop: the "one punch, everyone flies" slow-mo is instantly recognisable and fans already joke about
   its physics; pillow-goons in tiny caps are a cute, mute-friendly visual; "who flies last?" is a loop
   the viewer wants closed. The little-sister-is-the-real-boss twist feels "mana" and is very taggable.
2. The fight that stops for a punch dialogue: Chinna freezes mid-punch to deliver a long line, the
   pillows get bored and fall asleep. Funny, but the joke is "nothing happens" — a drag-risk structure we
   just used for the Ep 5 freeze. Dropped for variety.
3. The dramatic cycle stunt: Chinna attempts a slow-motion cycle jump over the sandbox and lands in it.
   Good visual, but a fall-on-a-bike gag risks looking like a real injury and needs a stunt set-up in
   every clip. Parked for a future Monday (with training wheels and a soft landing).

**Safety (non-negotiable):** "one punch, the goons fly in slow motion" is a generic action-movie trope —
no specific film, scene, fight choreography, dialogue, BGM, song or poster is referenced. Nobody is
hit: Chinna punches the **air**; the "rowdies" are inanimate pillows and cushions; Pinky's attack is a
single **puff of breath**; Chinna lands softly on the pillow pile, dazed and smiling, no injury. No
weapons, no blood, no aggressive contact. Chinna's lines ("నాకు ఒక్క punch చాలు", "Mass అంటే… ఇదే!")
are generic hero brags, not quotes. No signature gestures linked to any real actor (no beard-stroke,
collar-flick, hair-flip, thigh-slap mannerisms). Original cast only, home colony location (golden-hour
evening). Household props only: pillows, sofa cushions, a pink bunny-shaped pillow, plastic chairs.
Nothing religious or political, no flags or symbols, no real people, no regions or fan wars.

**Production:** 3D clips from Google Flow (owner, from `shots.json`); the art director adds
`image_prompt` / `video_prompt` on top of the locked cast descriptions — do not change them. The
pillow "rowdies" are plain pillows/cushions in assorted solid colours, each with a tiny black cap; **no
faces, no printed text or patterns** on them. Plant the pink bunny pillow clearly at the end of the row
in shot 2 (it pays off in shot 4). Shots 3 and 5 are real slow motion. Fallback 2D if no clips by morning.

## Shot-by-shot visual plan (one action per shot, ≤ 8 s each)
1. **HOOK — Gopi to camera.** Gopi in the playground, toy mic up, one hand raised "stop". Behind him,
   slightly out of focus: Pandu standing proudly in front of a row of ten pillows and sofa cushions
   propped up on plastic chairs and the sandbox edge, each pillow wearing a tiny black cap. Chinna faces
   them, red towel cape lifting in the breeze. Text hook on screen.
2. **The gang (set-up).** Pandu struts along the pillow row like a gang boss, arms folded, gap-toothed
   smirk, patting a cushion's cap. At the end of the row, clearly visible: a **pink bunny-shaped
   pillow** with a tiny cap. Chinna, unbothered, pulls his yellow toy sunglasses down onto his eyes and
   cracks his knuckles (just a flex, no contact). Pinky watches from the red slide, licking her lollipop.
3. **THE PUNCH — extreme slow motion.** Chinna winds up, towel cape swirling, dust and neem leaves
   lifting, and throws one big punch **into the empty air**. A soft whoosh ripples outward and all ten
   pillows fly up and away in slow motion, spinning, caps popping off, cushions tumbling over the
   swings. Pandu's own cap blows backwards off his head; his mouth falls open. The pink bunny pillow
   sails highest.
4. **Hero pose + the bunny lands (turn).** Pillows thud down softly all around. Chinna strikes a
   victory pose, fist up, cape settling. Then the pink bunny pillow plops down right at Pinky's feet,
   squashed, its tiny cap over one ear. Pinky slowly looks from the bunny to Chinna; her lollipop
   lowers; her eyes narrow. Ominous little breeze, pigtails lift.
5. **PAYOFF (visual) — one puff, the hero flies.** Pinky marches up to Chinna, rises on tiptoe and blows
   one tiny puff at him. In even bigger slow motion than shot 3, Chinna flies backwards, cape
   fluttering, sunglasses spinning off into the air, eyes wide, and lands softly on the big pile of
   pillows, sinking in with a dazed, dizzy smile. Pandu and Gopi stare, jaws dropped.
6. **CTA.** Foreground: Gopi to camera, toy mic up, pointing at the viewer, then a big thumbs-up.
   Background: Pinky sits on top of the pillow pile like a little queen, hugging her bunny pillow,
   licking her lollipop; Pandu respectfully hands her his black cap; Chinna's sneakers stick out of
   the pillows below, wiggling.

## Retention pass
- **0–2 s (scroll-past risk):** text hook "ఒక్క Punch… పది Rowdies!" + the visible pillow gang in tiny caps
  behind Gopi is an instant double-take ("rowdies… are pillows?"). Gopi's "last ఎవరు ఎగిరారో చూడండి"
  opens the loop closed in shot 5.
- **Shot 2 (~6–12 s, set-up dip):** classic drop point. Kept to one boast + one comeback, no back-story.
  The pink bunny pillow is planted in frame here — a small, fair clue for sharp-eyed viewers and the
  seed of the twist (not a random insert).
- **Shot 3 (~13–20 s, the climax everyone expects):** the countdown "ఒకటి, రెండు… పది!" is a real pattern
  interrupt that matches the trope — it counts the flying pillows, giving the slow-mo a rhythm so it
  never drags. Capped at ~7 s.
- **Shot 4 (~20–27 s, biggest drop risk):** after the big punch viewers may think "done" and swipe. Cut
  the victory pose to one line, then the bunny pillow landing at Pinky's feet + her ominous "నా bunny
  pillow…?" opens a fresh loop ("uh-oh, what will she do?") right when the first loop closes.
- **Shot 5 (payoff):** the reveal is visual (a tiny puff sends the mass hero flying in bigger slow
  motion than his own punch), so it lands on mute; Pinky's 1-word "ఫూ!" + Gopi's punchline, no filler.
- **Shot 6:** CTA ≤ 4 s; the background gag (Pandu hands Pinky his cap — new gang boss; Chinna's
  wiggling sneakers) rewards those still watching and loops back to the hook's "who flew last?"
