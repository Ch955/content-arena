# Brief: Quiz — Which came first?

## Angle
"You know both films — but which released first?" Each question slide shows two titles (A / B) and the star or
director they share — NO years on question slides (that would give it away). Answer slide: answer + release
month/year of both + one fun note. Same star within each pair → no fan war. Goal: comments ("4/4?").

## Pairs (easy → hard)
1. Vijay Deverakonda — A) Arjun Reddy B) Pelli Choopulu → **B) Pelli Choopulu** (Jul 2016) vs Arjun Reddy (Aug 2017).
   Note: Pelli Choopulu was his first lead role and won the National Award for Best Feature Film in Telugu
   (no ceremony year on slide).
2. Jr NTR + S. S. Rajamouli — A) Simhadri B) Student No: 1 → **B) Student No: 1** (Sep 2001) vs Simhadri (Jul 2003).
   Note: Student No: 1 was Rajamouli's first film as a director.
3. Prabhas — A) Chatrapathi B) Varsham → **B) Varsham** (Jan 2004, Sankranti) vs Chatrapathi (Sep 2005).
   Note: Varsham was Trisha's first Telugu release.
4. Nagarjuna, both 1989 — A) Shiva B) Geethanjali → **B) Geethanjali** (May 1989) vs Shiva (Oct 1989).
   Note: Geethanjali was Mani Ratnam's first Telugu film; Shiva was Ram Gopal Varma's first film as director.
Vary the answer letter on the slides if the template allows (all B above — the worker may swap A/B order per pair).
