## Plot-hole check (every twist must survive a rewatch)
- **Why is a Medchal man's wallet at Ameerpet?** Ramulu works the night shift (8 PM–8 AM) at Tower B, Hitech City;
  Ameerpet is the interchange on his way home. Nikhil finds it at ~8:40 AM on his way in. (Not spoken; the platform clock
  shows 8:40.)
- **Why didn't Nikhil recognise the ID photo?** That is the point: he walks past the guard every night looking at his
  phone (L3 memory insert shows it). He reads the *address* (what he needs), not the name or the face. He recognises the
  man only in uniform, at the door, when the man says his name.
- **How does Ramulu know Nikhil's name?** From Nikhil's badge "NIKHIL R" that he sees at the gate every night (badge
  visible from L1; Nikhil glances at it in L10).
- **Why not call someone?** The ID has no phone; the slip is an estimate with no hospital name or number (shown in
  L1). Viewers suggesting "call the hospital" in comments is welcome engagement (one sample bubble says it).
- **Does the demo timing work?** Found 8:40 at Ameerpet, ≈ 2 hrs to Medchal, demo 11:00 at Hitech City → missed.
- **Does the ending make B "wrong"?** No. We never say the booth would have failed; the take is about seeing people,
  not about which option was right. Both stay defensible; the promotion cost is real and left unresolved.
- Rewatch clues: name + photo on the ID (L1, ~1 s), same moustache at the gate saluting (L3 paper insert),
  "Baby K. Sravya" (same initial as K. Ramulu) on the slip, Nikhil's badge.
