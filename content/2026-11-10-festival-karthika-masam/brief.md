# Brief: Karthika Masam special (owner request, 10 Nov 2026, post ~7 PM)

## Angle
Festive, respectful, family-friendly. Gopi shows 4 traditions (deepam, early-morning snanam, Somavaram at Shiva
temples, vanabhojanam under the usiri tree), then a quiz: how many wicks in the Karthika Pournami deepam?
Pinky guesses wrong, countdown, answer in the same reel (365 — one for each day of the year). Then "మీ ఇంట్లో దీపం
ఎవరు వెలిగిస్తారు? comment", share to family groups, greetings. Practices phrased as tradition / belief; no
health or miracle claims; no exact start date on screen ("మొదలైంది").
