# Brief: Podupu #4 — the tailless bird (answer in the same reel)

## Angle
Picked from 3:
1. **"A bird with no tail and no wings that crosses towns"** — impossible-sounding challenge; viewer believes
   "a bird must fly with wings" → after: "a letter was our bird!" (chosen: contradiction in line 1, fair answer,
   strong nostalgia for anyone whose family wrote letters).
2. "Our grandmothers' riddle that Gen Z can't solve" — generational challenge; risks sounding like a put-down.
3. "Before WhatsApp, this bird carried every message" — gives the answer away too early.

Why they stop: the hook is a contradiction (no tail, no wings, still flies 90 ఆమడలు). Pinky's "Aeroplane!" is the
guess most viewers will have, and Gopi knocks it out (a plane has a tail — so does a pigeon), which keeps them
guessing. Clue 2 (postman) makes it fair. Payoff: ఉత్తరం + the why + a modern twist (WhatsApp vs this bird).

## Script shape
HOOK (contradiction) → HOLD (answer at the end) → RIDDLE recited + "ఆమడ" explained (value: old word, made easy) →
CLUE 1 → PINKY's wrong guess + poll tap + "కాదు!" → CLUE 2 → FOLLOW → COMMENT + 5 s countdown → Pinky begs →
REVEAL ఉత్తరం → WHY → TWIST (WhatsApp) → ✅ CHECK → SHARE + CLOSE. Script: `script.json`.

  ("what is it?") carries them through.
- Pattern interrupts (all tied to the riddle): the 3-option glowing poll being tapped, the red ✗ buzzer on
  "Aeroplane", the countdown with comment bubbles, the bird morphing into an envelope at the reveal.

  it flips to ✉️ only at the reveal.
