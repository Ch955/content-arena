## Plot-hole check (the twist must survive a rewatch)
- **If Kiran had the runbook, why does Suresh call Nikhil?** Kiran is new and on call for the first time (roster card in
  L2: "On-call today: Kiran (new)"); a P1 escalation makes a TL reach for "the module guy" by reflex. Nikhil's handover
  went to Kiran on Friday evening, after Suresh had logged off — Suresh simply didn't know.
- **Why the family group?** A classic wrong-chat send on Friday 6:42 PM (visible in L6). Amma mentions it only to
  complain that office follows him everywhere — the line works as comedy on first watch and as the clue on rewatch.
- **Is "neither A nor B" a cheat?** No. He *does* take the call (24 s), so it's not B; he doesn't do the work, so it's
  not A. The real choice was made on Friday — which is the point of the take. Viewers who said A or B aren't "wrong":
  the comment bubbles and Gopi never judge them.
- **Does the ending make A look bad?** No. The appraisal stays real; the resolution even helps it (a fixed P1 and a
  documented module). B isn't shamed either — Nanna's line is about the trap of being the only one, not about duty.
- **Does it really take 10 minutes?** Step 4 of a runbook written by the module owner — plausible. The "2 hrs" chip
  snapping back to "10 mins ✅" is the comic callback to L3.
- Rewatch clues: roster card "On-call today: Kiran (new)" (L2), the family-group message "Kiran — payments module
  runbook, step 1–7" (L6), and Amma saying the word "runbook" (L6).
