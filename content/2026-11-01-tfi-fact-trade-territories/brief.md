# Brief: Box Office 101 — Nizam, Ceded, Andhra

## Slides (6)
1. Hook — "Collection posters lo 'Nizam', 'Ceded' ante enti?" (Box Office 101)
2. 3 trade blocks: **Nizam** = Telangana districts; **Ceded** = Rayalaseema (Kurnool, Kadapa, Chittoor,
   Anantapur); **Andhra** = 6 areas: UA, East, West, Krishna, Guntur, Nellore.
3. Why "Ceded"? In 1800 the Nizam of Hyderabad ceded (handed over) these districts to the British East India
   Company — the old name stuck in the film trade. (Year only. Optional neutral line: the region took the name
   "Rayalaseema" in 1928 — no person named.)
4. Real example — RRR, first 27 days, AP + TS distributor share (trade estimate, source: Filmibeat):
   Nizam ₹110.02 Cr · Ceded ₹50.20 Cr · Andhra (6 areas) ₹104.51 Cr · Total ₹264.73 Cr.
5. The maths: Andhra = 34.32 + 15.87 + 12.99 + 17.83 + 14.36 + 9.14 = 104.51. Share of total: Nizam ≈ 42%,
   Ceded ≈ 19%, Andhra ≈ 39% (110.02/264.73 = 41.6%, 50.20/264.73 = 19.0%, 104.51/264.73 = 39.5%).
   Fun fact: in this estimate the single Nizam territory earned slightly more than the six Andhra areas combined.
