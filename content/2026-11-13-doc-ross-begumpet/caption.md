A Nobel Prize discovery happened in Begumpet! On 20 August 1897, at the Begumpet Military Hospital in Secunderabad, army doctor Ronald Ross (who had wanted to be a writer) found the malaria parasite in the stomach of a mosquito that had fed on a malaria patient. He completed the proof with birds in Calcutta in 1898 and won the 1902 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine. Ross was British but born in Almora: the first Nobel laureate born in India, 11 years before Tagore (1913), the first Indian laureate.
