# Brief: Rasam Comedy Ep 3 — "Fake Rich Pandu"

## Angle
Genre of the day (Friday): comedy classics. Spoof of the classic Telugu comedy-film trope of the
**fake-rich show-off** — the character who brags about cars and bungalows until someone asks for proof.
Pandu boasts about his "AC car" and "swimming pool"; the grand reveal behind the neem tree is a
cardboard-box car with Amma's table fan taped to it and a plastic bucket "pool". Amma wants her fan back.
Trope only — no real person, film, scene, dialogue or song. Original cast only, home colony location
(golden-hour evening). Production: 3D clips from Google Flow (owner, from `shots.json`); the art
director adds `image_prompt` / `video_prompt`. Fallback 2D if no clips by morning.

## Shot plan (one action per shot)
1. Gopi to camera, hook.
2. Pandu brags to Chinna and Pinky, chest out.
3. Chinna and Pinky cross arms — "show us proof".
4. Pandu leads them in a pompous slow-motion "rich walk" towards the neem tree.
5. **Reveal:** behind the tree, a cardboard-box "car" with a table fan taped on the front and a plastic
   bucket of water as the "pool"; Amma storms in with the ladle, pointing at her fan.
6. Amma carries the fan away while Pandu sulks in the box; Gopi to camera, punchline + CTA.
