# ఒక Nobel discovery… మన Begumpet లో! 🦟 Ronald Ross, 1897 | Reel Rasam

20 August 1897, Begumpet Military Hospital, Secunderabad: army doctor Ronald Ross found the malaria parasite in a mosquito's stomach. Nobel Prize 1902. British, born in Almora — the first Nobel laureate born in India, 11 years before Tagore. The building still stands in Begumpet: Osmania University's Sir Ronald Ross Institute of Parasitology.
