# Brief: Rasam Comedy Ep 4 — "Colony Raja-Rani"

## Angle
Genre of the day (Saturday): mythology/period — this episode spoofs **period-film tropes only**: the
court herald's "Maharaja!" entry, the king's grand announcement, the rival king's challenge, the
slow-motion war charge and the loyal-servant twist. The kids play raja-rani in the colony with household
props: Amma's flowered bedsheet as a royal cape, a steel cooking-pot lid as a shield, a rolled-newspaper
sword, a broom as a spear and a plain cereal-box paper crown. The twist: the loyal "servant" Pinky
switches sides and brings in the real ruler of the house, Lakshmi Amma, who wants her pot lid back because
the dal is boiling over.

**Three angles considered**
1. **Household-props epic** (chosen): *before* — "those grand royal-war scenes need crores of VFX";
   *after* — "a pot lid, a broom and Amma's bedsheet are enough… and the real queen of every Telugu home
   is Amma." Why they stop: instant recognition of the period-film beats (nostalgia, "mana" humour) plus
   the absurd prop visual in frame one.
2. Loyal-servant betrayal: centre the reel on Pinky's switch of sides. Strong twist, but alone it needs
   more set-up than 6 shots allow — kept as the beat right before the reveal.
3. Kid king's proclamation ("homework బంద్!"): very relatable, but a one-joke idea — kept as shot 3's
   proclamation.

**Safety (non-negotiable):** period/royal-court tropes only. No gods, religious figures, religious
practices, temples, ritual items or sacred characters from the epics; no real film scenes, dialogues,
costumes, BGM or songs; no star lookalikes or voice imitation. Crowns are plain paper/cardboard (no
religious or film-specific motifs); no flags or symbols. Trope only, original cast only, home colony
location (golden-hour evening). The words "Maharaja", "rajyam", "yuddham" are used in their everyday,
non-religious royal sense.

**Production:** 3D clips from Google Flow (owner, from `shots.json`); the art director adds
`image_prompt` / `video_prompt`. Episode props sit **on top of** the locked cast descriptions (e.g.
Chinna keeps his red towel cape and the flowered bedsheet is tied over it) — do not change the locked
descriptions. Fallback 2D if no clips by morning.

## Shot-by-shot visual plan (one action per shot, ≤ 8 s each)
1. **HOOK — Gopi to camera.** Gopi in the playground, toy mic up, one hand raised "stop". Behind him,
   slightly out of focus, Chinna and Pandu face off in their props: pot-lid shield vs broom spear. Text
   hook on screen.
2. **"Maharaja!" entry.** Pinky stands on the sandbox edge like a court herald, cupping her mouth with
   her lollipop held high like a staff. Chinna enters in slow motion down the red slide's steps with the
   flowered bedsheet cape billowing over his red towel, cereal-box paper crown, steel pot-lid shield on
   his arm, rolled-newspaper sword raised.
3. **Grand announcement + rival king.** Chinna seated on a plastic chair "throne" on top of the sandbox,
   sword pointed to the sky as he proclaims. Pandu bursts in from the swings, broom held like a spear,
   a plastic plate tucked in his belt, pointing the broom at the red slide (the disputed "kingdom").
4. **Slow-motion war charge / palace sword fight.** Both charge at each other across the sandbox in
   exaggerated slow motion: sand flying, bedsheet cape flapping, newspaper sword meeting broom bristles,
   leaves drifting in golden light. Freeze just before the clash.
5. **REVEAL — the loyal-servant twist and the real ruler.** Pinky steps aside like a servant bowing in a
   new ruler; Lakshmi Amma storms onto the "battlefield", steel ladle raised like a sceptre, one hand
   grabbing the pot lid off Chinna's arm, eyes on the bedsheet. Chinna and Pandu freeze mid-charge,
   mouths open, weapons drooping.
6. **PUNCHLINE + CTA.** Background: Amma marches off with the pot lid in one hand, the bedsheet over her
   shoulder and the ladle, Pinky skipping proudly behind her. Chinna stands with his paper crown askew,
   Pandu bowing with the broom. Foreground: Gopi to camera, laughing, thumbs-up, punchline + "Follow
   Reel Rasam!"
