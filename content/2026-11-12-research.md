## 1) Podupu katha: తోకలేని పిట్ట … → ఉత్తరం (letter)

### Answer: ఉత్తరం (a letter / post). Every source agrees.
- Eenadu Pratibha (DSC Telugu lesson: సామెతలు, జాతీయాలు, పొడుపు కథలు). It explains the riddle and gives the answer
  ఉత్తరం. Search-returned gloss: *"ఇక్కడ తొంభై ఆమడలు అంటే చాలా దూరం అని అర్థం. దీనికి సమాధానం - ''ఉత్తరం''."*
  It also says a letter is compared to a bird, and calling it "tailless" is the riddle's imaginative twist.
  https://pratibha.eenadu.net/jobs/lesson/dsc/trt/dsc-telangana/Telugu-Medium/education/2-1-8-836-1278-1647-12806-20321-7867-23040002923
- GoTelugu, "పొడుపు కథలు" by Dr. బెల్లంకొండ నాగేశ్వరరావు. It lists the riddle with the answer ఉత్తరం.
  https://www.gotelugu.com/teluguarticles/view/11413/
- Telugu Kiranaalu blog (పొడుపు కథలు). Answer ఉత్తరం, **but the distance in this version is "తొంభై కోసులు"** (kos, not ఆమడ).
  http://telugukiranaalu.blogspot.com/2012/07/blog-post_265.html
- Other collections that came up for the same search (not individually confirmed): https://gvdngo.blogspot.com/2010/09/blog-post.html ,
  https://www.chaibisket.com/blogs/blog/telugu-podupu-kathalu
- Side note: "తోకలేని పిట్ట" is also the title of a Telugu story about a postman, which shows the phrase is
  commonly understood to mean a letter: https://www.manatelugukathalu.com/post/tokaleni-pitta-postman-shivan-telugu-story-7105

### What is an ఆమడ? (traditional distance unit)
- Lokal app article "'ఆమడ దూరం' అంటే అర్థం ఇదే": **ఆమడ = 8 మైళ్ళు ≈ 13 km**. It says this was the largest unit
  used before the British arrived and that it is also called యోజనం.
  https://telugu.getlokalapp.com/telangana-news/this-is-what-sitting-distance-means-13945008
- GoTelugu "నాటి తూనికలు - కొలతలు": says it was roughly **standardised at ~10 miles under British rule**, and
  1 యోజనం = 4 పరువులు / 4 క్రోశాలు. https://www.gotelugu.com/teluguarticles/view/11752/
- Telugu Quora (not a reliable source, context only): ఆమడ = యోజనం, 8 or 10 miles.
  https://te.quora.com/%E0%B0%86%E0%B0%AE%E0%B0%A1-%E0%B0%A6%E0%B1%82%E0%B0%B0%E0%B0%82-%E0%B0%85%E0%B0%A8%E0%B1%87-%E0%B0%B5%E0%B1%8D%E0%B0%AF%E0%B0%95%E0%B1%8D%E0%B0%A4%E0%B1%80%E0%B0%95%E0%B0%B0%E0%B0%A3%E0%B0%B2%E0%B1%8B-1
- The yojana value itself varies by source. Oxford Reference gives popular conversions of 2.5, 4.5 or 9 miles:
  https://www.oxfordreference.com/display/10.1093/oi/authority.20110803125349759 . Merriam-Webster says 4–10 miles,
  commonly about 5: https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/yojan
- **Status:** "ఆమడ = about 8 miles (~13 km)" comes from popular articles, not scholarly metrology, and the
  ఆమడ = యోజనం equivalence is likewise popular-source only. **Safest on-screen wording:** "ఆమడ = పాతకాలపు దూరం
  కొలత (సుమారు 8 మైళ్ళు అని చెబుతారు)" or just "చాలా దూరం", as the Eenadu gloss does. Any exact figure → mark
  "approx., traditional unit; values vary". Owner decides.

### Arithmetic (only if a number is shown)
- 8 mi × 1.609 km/mi = 12.87 km ≈ 13 km ✔ (matches the Lokal figure)
- 90 ఆమడలు × 8 mi = 720 mi; 720 × 1.609 = 1,158.5 km ≈ **~1,160 km** ✔ (90 × 12.87 = 1,158.3, the same to rounding)
- At the 10-mile value: 90 × 10 = 900 mi ≈ 1,448 km (900 × 1.609 = 1,448.1) ✔
- Because the unit's value varies (and 90 is figurative, "very far"), I recommend **not** putting a km figure on screen.

## 2) Life lesson: Sumathi Satakam, తన కోపమె తన శత్రువు

### Text (kanda padyam, 4 lines). Source text CHECK-ON-PAGE
Search results quote the verse from Dharmsutra and Vignanam as one run of text:
> తన కోపమె తన శత్రువు, తన శాంతమె తనకు రక్ష, దయ చుట్టంబౌ తన సంతోషమె స్వర్గము, తన దుఃఖమె నరక మండ్రు తథ్యము సుమతీ ॥ 50 ॥

Set in its four kanda lines:

```
తన కోపమె తన శత్రువు
తన శాంతమె తనకు రక్ష దయ చుట్టంబౌ
తన సంతోషమె స్వర్గము
తన దుఃఖమె నరకమండ్రు తథ్యము సుమతీ!
```

### Word meanings (our own gloss; owner to review Telugu, rule 7)
కోపము = anger · శత్రువు = enemy · శాంతము = calm, patience · రక్ష = protection · దయ = kindness, compassion ·
చుట్టము (చుట్టంబౌ = becomes a kinsman) = relative · సంతోషము = contentment, happiness · స్వర్గము = heaven ·
దుఃఖము = sorrow · నరకము = hell · అండ్రు = "they say" · తథ్యము = certain truth · సుమతీ = O wise one (the satakam's refrain).

### Meaning (our own words, consistent with the Kavithalu.in / Wikisource gloss reported by search)
**తెలుగు:** మన కోపమే మనకు శత్రువు. మన శాంతమే మనల్ని కాపాడే రక్ష. మనం చూపే దయ మనకు బంధువు లాంటిది.
మన సంతోషమే మనకు స్వర్గం, మన దుఃఖమే మనకు నరకం. ఇది నిజం, ఓ సుమతీ!

**English:** Your own anger is your enemy; your own calm is your protection; kindness is like a relative who
stands by you. Your own contentment is heaven and your own sorrow is hell, as the wise say. This is the truth, O wise one.

(The lesson's core idea is that heaven and hell are inner states we create. That idea is in the verse itself. Any
modern framing, such as Stoic parallels, would need its own source.)

  according to many literary critics", and describes him as a Chola prince and vassal under Kakatiya ruler Rani
  Rudrama Devi. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sumathi_Satakam
  (reached via search, mirror shown: https://wikipedia.com/wiki/Sumathi_Satakam)
- **Attribution is traditional, not certain.** Wikipedia's own wording ("reputedly", "according to many critics")
