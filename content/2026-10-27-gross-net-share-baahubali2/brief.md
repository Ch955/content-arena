# Brief: Gross vs Net vs Share

## Why now
Collection headlines for every big release quote "₹X crore" without saying
which number it is. Evergreen explainer; re-usable whenever a big Telugu
release's numbers trend.

## Angle
"₹327.9 Cr gross — but distributors got only ₹203.65 Cr. Where did the rest go?"
Use one real, well-known hit and walk the money from ticket counter to distributor.

## Key points
1. **Gross** — everything audiences paid at the counter, taxes included.
2. **Net** — gross minus taxes.
3. **Share** — net minus the theatres' cut; what reaches the distributor.
4. Example: Baahubali 2, AP/Nizam lifetime — gross ₹327.9 Cr → net ₹295.3 Cr → share ₹203.65 Cr (≈62% of gross).
5. Takeaway: always ask which number a headline is quoting.

## Core script (Tenglish, ~45 s)
- Baahubali 2 — Telugu states lo gross ₹327.9 crores vachindi. Kani distributors chethiki entha vachindo telusa?
- Gross ante — theatre counter daggara audience kattina total amount, taxes tho kalipi.
- Daantlo taxes teesesthe ₹32.6 crores poyindi. Migilindi ₹295.3 crores — idhe Net.
- Ippudu theatres vaalla share teeskuntaru — ₹91.65 crores. Distributors ki vachindi ₹203.65 crores matrame. Idhe Share.
- Ante, gross lo almost 62 percent matrame share ga vachindi!
- Next time collection news chusinappudu — adi gross aa, net aa, share aa ani check cheyandi.
