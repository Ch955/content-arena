# Brief: Life lesson — Sumathi Satakam on anger

Thu 12 Nov 2026, slot 2 (life-lesson). Cast: Gopi (host) + Pinky. Art style: **clay**. Target ~45 s; with the
slow recitation and the 5 s countdown expect ~55–65 s (the Vemana reel ran 80 s with 16 lines) — trim gaps in
`voices.py` if it runs long.

## Angle
- **Option 1 (chosen):** belief "my enemy is someone outside who wrongs me" → after "my own anger hurts me first;
  10 seconds of calm protects me". Stops the scroll because "your biggest enemy is right next to you" is a
  contradiction everyone wants resolved, and the broken-toy moment is something every viewer (and parent) has lived.
- Option 2: "Heaven and hell are inside you" (సంతోషమె స్వర్గము). Deep, but abstract for a 45 s reel, and too close to
  religious talk.
- Option 3: "Shouting wins arguments?" A debate hook. Useful, but it loses the poem's simple warmth.

## Poem (Sumathi Satakam, kanda padyam; no verse number on screen — editions number it 50 or 46)
తన కోపమె తన శత్రువు / తన శాంతమె తనకు రక్ష దయ చుట్టంబౌ /
తన సంతోషమె స్వర్గము / తన దుఃఖమె నరకమండ్రు తథ్యము సుమతీ!
Meaning: your anger is your own enemy; your calm protects you; kindness is like your own relative; your
contentment is heaven, your sorrow is hell — this is certain.
Attribution: "traditionally attributed to Baddena (c. 13th century)" — **caption only**, never as certain, not spoken.

## Retention pass
- **Drop risk 1, the poem (L4–L5):** slow recitation of old Telugu. Fix: the lines appear on a clay scroll as
  they're spoken, each phrase gets a visual (heart-shield, linked hands, sun cloud vs rain cloud), and the plain
  meaning follows immediately in 2 short lines.
- **Drop risk 2, the follow aside (L8):** kept to one quick line, and the dilemma is teased right after ("ఇప్పుడు మీకు ఒక test!").
- **Drop risk 3, the 5 s countdown:** real pattern interrupt — comment bubbles pop, the countdown is big, and
  Pinky slams the A button right after (comic beat) before the reveal.
- Cut: no intro about the poet, no year, no verse number, no extra moral after the why.
