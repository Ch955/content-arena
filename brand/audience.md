# Reel Rasam — audience card (read before every script)

**Niche:** Telugu cinema & OTT — comedy spoofs of TFI tropes, OTT picks, box-office explainers, movie quizzes.
**Audience:** Telugu movie lovers, 16–35, AP/Telangana + Telugu diaspora; watch on phone, mostly Tenglish;
fans who rewatch mass scenes, argue about collections, and ask "ee week emi chudali?".
**Language/tone:** natural Tenglish, talking *to* the viewer like a friend at a tea stall — playful, never preachy.

## What they want / ask / get wrong (use their words in hooks)
- Wants: something to watch tonight without scrolling 20 minutes; to sound smart in fan debates; a laugh
  that feels "mana" (ours); to be first to know.
- Asks: "Ee week OTT lo emi vastundi?", "Telugu lo undha?", "Gross ante enti? Share ante enti?",
  "Hit aa flop aa?", "Ee dialogue ye cinema lodi?"
- Gets wrong: thinks gross = profit; trusts fake collection posters; misses dubbed gems; thinks a movie is on
  OTT when it's rent-only.
- Frustrations: clickbait thumbnails, fan-war negativity, long intros, fake numbers.
- Emotional core: belonging (fan identity), nostalgia (classic tropes), FOMO (new releases), pride in TFI.
