Gross = what audiences paid at the counter (taxes included).
Net = gross minus taxes.
Share = net minus the theatres' cut — what reaches the distributor.

Next time you see a collections headline, ask: gross, net or share? 🤔

Data: Andhra Box Office via IBTimes India (20 Sep 2017), AP/Nizam lifetime. Trade estimates, not audited figures.
