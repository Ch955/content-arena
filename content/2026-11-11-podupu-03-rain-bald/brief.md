# Brief: Podupu #3 — the rain riddle (answer in the same reel)

## Angle
A man walks home in heavy rain: no umbrella, no cap, no raincoat, he gets fully wet — but not one hair on his head
gets wet. How? Pinky guesses "raincoat?" (✗ — we said no raincoat), countdown, answer: he is bald (బట్టతల) — light,
friendly, no mocking (the man laughs along). A classic lateral-thinking riddle retold with our own characters.
